Brooklyn Bridge — material and arrangement evidence — photo review

Give each photograph every category it can actually inform — one view of a tower is often masonry, a cornice and a cable anchorage at once. Tick skip for anything not worth keeping. Untouched photographs stay auto_screened and are treated as weaker evidence.
A photograph can establish what something is made of and how it is arranged. It cannot give a dimension without scale control. Tick a category for what the image shows, not for what you hope to measure from it.
masonry
Tower or anchorage stonework close enough to tell granite from limestone, and to see the coursing SRC-002 describes. Feeds MAT-002 and MAT-003.
arcade
The approach viaduct from below or beside. OQ-007: the model draws slender bents, but SRC-004 describes brick piers and arches. This is the category that settles what actually carries the approach.
cornice
The tower top above the saddle. OQ-016: the model opens the masonry across the cable corridor because SRC-002 says the cables pass THROUGH the towers, but nothing dimensions the opening.
saddle
Where a main cable crosses a tower. Shows how much of the cable is visible above the masonry -- the thing that looked wrong in the render.
truss
A transverse or oblique view of the stiffening trusses. OQ-002: their spacing across the 85 ft deck is a placeholder. A photograph cannot measure it, but it can show how many there are and roughly where they sit.
cable
Main cable, suspenders, diagonal stays, cable bands. Wrapping, galvanising and the stay arrangement that SRC-002 annotates.
deck
The roadway surface and its framing. MAT-009 is graded D because no read source says what the wearing surface is.
promenade
The walkway decking. MAT-010 is D even though every photograph shows timber, because 'wood deck' in SRC-011 is a TYPOLOGY NAME and not a materials statement. A reviewed photograph is what changes that honestly.
stair
The Washington Street stair down to DUMBO, or the Manhattan approach stair. Their location is sourced; their form is not. OQ-015.
railing
Promenade railings, fencing and the balustrade. Not yet modelled at all.
lighting
Lamp standards on the promenade. Not modelled; the brief lists them under details.
context
A good overall view that shows the bridge in its setting but settles no single component. Useful for the compare panel, weak as evidence.
